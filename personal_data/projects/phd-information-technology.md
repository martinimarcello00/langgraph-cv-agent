# PhD in Information Technology at Politecnico di Milano

Marcello Martini is a PhD Student in Information Technology at Politecnico di Milano, IT, starting in May 2026.

## Research Topic
His research focus is on **Multi-Agent Systems for autonomous observability and self-healing cloud ecosystems**.

### Overview
This research aims to build state-of-the-art architectures using collaborative, autonomous AI agents to automate system monitoring, logging, and self-healing. By leveraging Multi-Agent Systems (such as LangGraph), the cloud ecosystems can autonomously diagnose failures, apply self-healing protocols, and maintain highly-observable distributed infrastructures with zero-touch operation.
